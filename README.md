# HealthSarthi AI

## About
HealthSarthi AI is an AI-powered rural healthcare assistant designed to help people identify possible health risks early and access basic healthcare guidance in local languages.

## Problem
Millions of people in rural India face delays in diagnosis due to limited access to doctors and healthcare facilities.

## Solution
HealthSarthi AI provides symptom assessment, health guidance, emergency alerts, and information about nearby healthcare facilities.

## Features
- AI Symptom Checker
- Health Tips
- Local Language Support
- Emergency Guidance
- Nearby Health Centers
